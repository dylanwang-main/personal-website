# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

General audience — anyone curious about Dylan Wang. No specific professional gate. The site doubles as creative showcase: the craft of the experience itself is part of the message.

## Product Purpose

A personal website that expresses who Dylan Wang is through an interactive museum metaphor. The museum format is the point — the medium is the message. Visitors walk through a curated sequence of exhibits rather than scrolling a page. The site makes personality, taste, and creative range visible in ways a LinkedIn or GitHub profile cannot.

## Positioning

Not a portfolio site wearing a theme. The museum experience — entrance, door, hall, exhibits, exit — is the product. The interaction design, transitions, and spatial narrative are the differentiator.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive (likely from a link or search), experience the entrance, push through the animated door, browse five framed paintings in the hall, enter exhibit rooms, and exit. One-way forward navigation is intentional: entrance → door → hall → exhibit → exit. Header nav provides cross-navigation as the deliberate escape hatch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

### Fixed structure
- Five exhibit rooms, locked: Projects, Currently Building, Content Creation, Hobbies, Travels
- Content Creation is the featured exhibit (gold frame); the other four use neutral frame tones
- One-way navigation path is intentional, not a gap
- Museum metaphor is sacred — must survive any future redesign

### Visual commitments
- Ornate detailed picture frames throughout
- Gold accent reserved for the featured exhibit frame only — not a site-wide warm accent
- Glassmorphism header: kept, restrained/subtle only, not heavy full-blur
- Type pairing must read premium/expensive, not generic SaaS. Cinzel + Raleway current but not locked. Inter or geometric sans forbidden as primary typeface
- No numbered section markers (01/02/03 style labels)
- No cream/terracotta palette direction

### Content status
All copy, images, social links, and exhibit content are placeholder. Structure is complete; real content has not been filled in.

## Brand Commitments

- Site identity is "Dylan Wang" directly. No separate site name, brand, or "Digital Museum" label
- No logo — text name treatment only

## Evidence on Hand

No real content, images, testimonials, or case studies yet. All exhibit copy is placeholder. Future work must not fabricate claims, credentials, or testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. **The medium is the message.** The museum experience itself demonstrates creative and technical ability — it is not decoration on top of content.
2. **Restraint is luxury.** Premium feel comes from precise details and intentional constraint, not from adding more. Every ornament earns its place.
3. **Forward momentum.** The one-way path creates narrative — visitors are guided through a sequence, not dropped into a grid.
4. **The frame elevates the work.** Ornate frames, transitions, and spatial metaphor exist to make the content inside feel significant, not to compete with it.
5. **Craft over convention.** Reject portfolio-site patterns (hero → grid → footer) in favor of choices that serve the museum concept specifically.
