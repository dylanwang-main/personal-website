## Surface brief: app (whole site)

Mode: Experience
Audience: Anyone curious about Dylan Wang, arriving from a link or search
Job: Form an impression of taste and capability within 2 minutes
Action: Walk through the museum, experience the work
Constraints: 5 rooms, one-way path, museum metaphor, featured exhibit (Content Creation)

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dark cinematic vault where content is revealed by light, not by layout. The category default — warm cream portfolio with serif headings — is refused entirely. Every surface is near-black; light is the event.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black walls (#0a0a0b–#0e0e10), warm off-white text (#e8e6e1), champagne gold accent, gunmetal/silver ornate frames. Cinzel display tightened and scaled large. Body face sharp and editorial. SVG noise grain at low opacity. Directional spotlights on paintings. Frosted dark glass on overlays.

STORY: Visitor arrives in darkness. Name appears as a title sequence — cinematic fade, not a door. The scene opens to reveal the hall: spotlit paintings on dark walls. Gold frame glows. They choose an exhibit, the frame flies and lands, content reveals in light. They leave through a closing sequence back to black.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Near-black field fills the screen. The name "Dylan Wang" appears large, centered, in Cinzel at architectural scale, warm off-white on darkness. Taglines cycle below in the body face. Below the name: a call to action — "Enter" — minimal, gold-accented. No door, no slab, no architrave. The threshold is a cinematic iris/push, not a physical door.

FORM: Cinematic title sequence entrance, spotlit dark gallery hall, light-revealed exhibit rooms. User-pinned, no seed key.

FINISH: Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
