## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** 8.5/10. Genuinely authored, not a themed template. Metaphor is load-bearing architecture. Thins at footer and exit.

**Deterministic scan:** 0 findings. Clean on all mechanical rules.

## Overall Impression

Remarkable commitment to a museum world. Frame component, motion system, and empty-state design are excellent craft. Edges (footer, exit, entrance scroll conflict) don't match the quality of the middle.

## What's Working

1. OrnateFrame: four physical layers, one light source, corner studs, gold/neutral boolean. Trustworthy design component.
2. Motion system: named constants, semantic ease curves, derived delays. Coherent and documented.
3. Empty-state metaphor: primed linen canvas with artwork title — museum-native, not "Coming soon."

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Footer breaks the museum world
Standard portfolio footer on every page. No museum vocabulary. "© 2026 Dylan Wang" after a breathing door is a conceptual full stop.

### [P1] Entrance cinema/scroll conflict
Page is both cinematic gate and landing page. Enter button scrolls off. Room teasers below fold spoil surprise. "The door is the only way in" contradicted by Floor Plan direct-links.

### [P2] Exit copy is the weakest text on the site
"Thanks for visiting. That concludes the walkthrough." Peak-end rule makes this critical. ExitGlow ceremony good; copy neutralizes it.

### [P2] No clickability affordance on hall paintings (critical on mobile)
Only signal is hover:scale-[1.04]. Mobile has no hover. Zero affordance for primary navigation gesture.

### [P2] FloorPlan modal has no focus trap
Keyboard users Tab out of dialog. Background not aria-hidden during modal. WCAG 2.4.3 violation.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer):** On mobile, zero affordance that paintings are doors. Hall subtitle reads like tooltip, not wayfinding.

**Sam (Accessibility):** FloorPlan has no focus trap. ArrivalCurtain decouples visual/auditory experience. Footer links unreachable by keyboard. Muted color likely below 4.5:1 contrast.
